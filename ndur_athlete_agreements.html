Coaching Services Agreement

Between Ndur ("Coach") and the athlete completing this form ("Athlete")

1. Services

Ndur agrees to provide personalized marathon training coaching services to the Athlete, which may include but are not limited to:

  • A customized monthly training plan based on the Athlete's current fitness, goals, and race schedule
  • Regular plan updates and adjustments based on Athlete feedback and progress
  • Communication via nic@ndur.life for questions and check-ins
  • Race strategy and pacing guidance

The specific tier of service (plan only, full coaching, or race strategy session) will be agreed upon separately prior to the commencement of services.

2. Term & Billing

Services are billed on a monthly basis. The agreement begins on the date the Athlete's intake form is submitted and first payment is received, and continues month-to-month until cancelled by either party.

  • Payment is due on the 1st of each month
  • Either party may cancel with 14 days written notice
  • No refunds will be issued for partial months already in progress
  • Payments are processed via Stripe

3. Athlete Responsibilities

The Athlete agrees to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information on the intake form and keep it updated
  • Communicate any injuries, illnesses, or significant life changes that may affect training
  • Obtain medical clearance from a licensed physician before beginning any training program
  • Follow the training plan as prescribed to the best of their ability, and communicate when adjustments are needed
  • Respond to coach communications within 48 hours to maintain an effective coaching relationship

4. Coach Responsibilities

Ndur agrees to:

  • Provide a training plan within 2 business days of receiving payment
  • Respond to Athlete questions and check-ins within 2 business days
  • Adjust the training plan as needed based on Athlete progress and feedback
  • Keep all Athlete information confidential in accordance with the Privacy Policy

5. Not Medical Advice

Coaching services provided by Ndur are fitness and performance coaching only and do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The Athlete should consult a qualified physician before beginning any training program and for any health concerns that arise during training.

6. Intellectual Property

All training plans, materials, and content created by Ndur are proprietary and intended for the Athlete's personal use only. Athlete may not share, distribute, resell, or publish coaching materials without written consent from Ndur.

7. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of South Dakota. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Sioux Falls, South Dakota.

Document 02

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6.12.26 · Ndur is committed to protecting the privacy of its athletes.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete the Ndur intake form, we collect the following personal information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Age and gender
  • Running experience, current weekly mileage, and training history
  • Goal race and race date
  • Any additional health or fitness information you voluntarily provide

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Build and personalize your training plan
  • Communicate with you about your training and coaching services
  • Process payments for coaching services
  • Improve our coaching methods over time (in aggregate and anonymized form only)

3. How We Store Your Information

Your personal data is stored securely using Supabase, a GDPR-compliant database platform with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Access to your data is restricted to Ndur coaching staff only.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes, ever.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for the duration of your coaching relationship with Ndur, plus 2 years after your last active coaching period. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at nic@ndur.life.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nic@ndur.life.

6. Communications

By submitting the intake form, you consent to receiving coaching-related communications from Ndur via email. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe."

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active athletes of any material changes by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to: Nicholas DeSchepper, Ndur, nic@ndur.life

Liability Waiver & Release

Please read carefully before signing. This is a legally binding document.

Important: By signing this waiver, you are giving up certain legal rights, including the right to sue for injuries or damages. Read fully before agreeing.

1. Assumption of Risk

I, the undersigned Athlete, acknowledge and understand that marathon training and distance running involve inherent risks, including but not limited to:

  • Musculoskeletal injuries including strains, sprains, stress fractures, and overuse injuries
  • Cardiovascular events including abnormal heart rhythms and, in rare cases, cardiac arrest
  • Heat-related illness, dehydration, and hyponatremia
  • Falls, accidents, and injuries caused by road, trail, or weather conditions
  • Psychological stress associated with high-volume training

I voluntarily assume all such risks, whether known or unknown, and accept full responsibility for any injury, illness, or loss that may result from participation in a training program provided by Ndur.

2. Medical Clearance

I confirm that I have consulted with, or will consult with, a licensed physician prior to beginning this training program. I represent that I am in suitable physical condition to undertake marathon training and am not aware of any medical condition that would make participation inadvisable. I agree to immediately notify Ndur and seek medical attention if I experience any unusual symptoms during training.

3. Release of Liability

In consideration of Ndur providing coaching services to me, I hereby release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue Ndur, its owners, coaches, employees, agents, and affiliates (collectively, "Released Parties") from any and all claims, demands, damages, rights of action, or causes of action arising from:

  • Participation in any training activities prescribed or recommended by Ndur
  • Any injury, illness, death, or property damage resulting from such participation
  • The negligence of the Released Parties, to the fullest extent permitted by law

4. Indemnification

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any loss, liability, damage, or cost they may incur arising out of or related to my participation in training activities, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

5. Emergency Medical Authorization

In the event of an emergency where I am unable to provide consent, I authorize Ndur to seek emergency medical assistance on my behalf. I agree to be responsible for all costs associated with such medical care.

6. Acknowledgment of Independent Judgment

I acknowledge that I am free to modify, reduce, or stop any prescribed training at any time based on my own physical condition and judgment. Ndur's training recommendations are guidance only, and I retain sole responsibility for decisions about my own body and training.

7. Severability

If any provision of this waiver is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

8. Voluntary Agreement

I have read this Liability Waiver and Release in its entirety, I understand its contents, and I sign it voluntarily and of my own free will. I am at least 18 years of age, or if a minor, my parent or legal guardian has signed on my behalf.
